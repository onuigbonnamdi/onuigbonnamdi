**Founder and AI Systems Engineer** at [Evervia Innovations Ltd](https://evervia.co.uk) and [SmartFlow Lab Ltd](https://smartflowlab.dev).

I'm an electrical and electronic engineer specialising in power and sustainable energy, and I build AI systems. I design, ship, and maintain production systems end to end: data pipelines, machine learning models, APIs, LLM integrations, automation workflows, and the applications people use.

## What I'm building

**Evervia (B2B) and GridSense (B2C):** AI energy intelligence for UK organisations and households. Live grid data, demand forecasting, property energy profiles from 12M+ EPC records, and LLM-powered bill analysis that compares what users pay against their property's EPC baseline and the Ofgem price cap, and flags the best times to use energy.

- **48-hour UK demand forecasting:** Random Forest, R² = 0.984 and MAE = 399 MW under time-series cross-validation, trained on 18 months of data with weekly retraining
- Live at [gridsense.evervia.co.uk](https://gridsense.evervia.co.uk)

**[SmartFlow Lab](https://smartflowlab.dev):** AI automation for businesses, including a content engine that writes, designs, and publishes LinkedIn posts with human review and fact-checking guardrails.
